Where the data comes from

Each competition comes from the site or registration page its organiser publishes, linked from its page with the date it was checked. If you organise one and something is off, write to us and we will correct or remove it.

CrossFit, HYROX, DEKA and Spartan are registered trademarks of their owners. CatchUp Heats is not affiliated with them: it only names them to say what each competition is.

Privacy

This site uses no cookies and asks you for no data. If it counts visits, the analytics are anonymous: page views only, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you.

It counts visits with Umami, with no cookies, its data kept in the European Union, and not counting visitors whose browser asks not to be tracked.

The map is drawn with OpenFreeMap: when you open it, your browser asks its server for the map images, and that server sees your IP address like any site you visit. It uses no cookies.

Organising one of these competitions? Write to us to correct or remove it.
